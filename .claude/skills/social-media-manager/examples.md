## Hook Formula Breakdown

### Pattern Interrupt Hooks:
- "Everyone says X. I did the opposite. Here's what happened:"
- "Forget everything you know about X"
- "X is broken. Here's the better way:"

### Curiosity Gap Hooks:
- "I discovered why 90% of people fail at X"
- "After analyzing 1,000 X, I found the pattern"
- "The thing nobody tells you about X"

### Bold Claim Hooks:
- "This one change 10x'd my X"
- "X is dead. Y is the future."
- "I cracked the code to X. Here's how:"

### Relatability Hooks:
- "If you've ever felt X, read this"
- "3 years ago I was X. Today I'm Y. Here's the shift:"
- "You're not failing at X. X is failing you."

### Data/Social Proof Hooks:
- "I spent $10k learning X. Here's what worked:"
- "I interviewed 100 successful X. They all do this:"
- "After 10 years of X, here are the only 3 things that matter:"

---

Each thread should:
✓ Hook in first 280 characters
✓ Deliver value in middle tweets
✓ End with CTA or question
✓ Use line breaks for readability
✓ Include 1-2 emojis max
✓ Be RT-able as individual tweets
